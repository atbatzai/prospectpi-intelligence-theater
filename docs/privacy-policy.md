# ProspectPI Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** December 29, 2025  
**Version:** 1.0.0

## Overview

ProspectPI Intelligence Theater provides business-to-business (B2B) intelligence services that help sales teams research and understand potential prospects. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## What Data We Collect

### Essential Business Information
- **Contact Information:** Email address, first name, last name
- **Account Information:** Organization affiliation, role, subscription details
- **Usage Data:** Research requests, dossier generation history, API usage patterns

### Technical Information  
- **Session Data:** Login times, IP addresses (for security), browser information
- **Audit Logs:** System access logs for security and compliance monitoring

## What We DON'T Collect

- **Personal Financial Information:** Credit cards processed by Stripe (PCI compliant)
- **Sensitive Personal Data:** No health, biometric, or personal lifestyle data
- **Individual Consumer Data:** We research companies, not individuals

## How We Use Your Data

### Primary Business Uses
1. **Service Delivery:** Generate business intelligence dossiers about companies
2. **Account Management:** Manage subscriptions, usage limits, and billing
3. **Security & Compliance:** Audit trails, fraud prevention, regulatory compliance
4. **Service Improvement:** Usage analytics to improve platform performance

### Legal Basis (GDPR)
- **Contractual Necessity:** Account management and service delivery
- **Legitimate Business Interest:** Security, fraud prevention, service improvement
- **Consent:** Marketing communications (opt-in only)

## Your Rights & Controls

### Data Subject Rights
- **Right to Access:** Export all your data via API endpoint `/api/v1/privacy/export`
- **Right to Correction:** Update account information through user settings
- **Right to Deletion:** Request account deletion via `/api/v1/privacy/account`
- **Right to Portability:** Download your data in JSON format
- **Consent Management:** Control marketing and analytics preferences

### How to Exercise Rights
1. **Self-Service:** Use privacy API endpoints or dashboard settings
2. **Support Request:** Email privacy@prospectpi.com
3. **Response Time:** 30 days maximum, typically within 7 days

## Data Retention

### Retention Periods
- **Active Account Data:** Retained while account is active
- **Audit Logs:** 90 days for security compliance
- **Deleted Account Data:** 30-day grace period, then permanent deletion
- **Business Intelligence:** Company research (non-personal) retained indefinitely

### Automated Cleanup
- Expired sessions automatically deleted
- Audit logs automatically purged after 90 days
- Inactive trial accounts deleted after 60 days

## Regional Data Processing

### Data Residency
- **US Users:** Data processed in US data centers
- **EU Users:** Data processed in EU data centers (GDPR compliance)
- **UK Users:** Data processed in UK data centers (UK-GDPR compliance)
- **Canadian Users:** Data processed in Canadian data centers (PIPEDA compliance)

### Cross-Border Transfers
- Data transfers outside your region only with appropriate safeguards
- EU users: Standard Contractual Clauses for any US transfers
- Automatic regional routing based on user preferences

## Security Measures

### Technical Safeguards
- **Encryption:** All data encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256)
- **Authentication:** Secure password hashing (bcrypt), JWT session management
- **Access Control:** Role-based permissions, audit logging
- **Infrastructure:** SOC2-compliant hosting, regular security audits

### Organizational Safeguards
- Employee privacy training
- Regular security assessments
- Incident response procedures
- Data breach notification within 72 hours (GDPR requirement)
