#  Story 10.2: Regional Business Intelligence

**Epic:** 10 - Global Market Intelligence  
**Priority:** P1  
**Story Points:** 15  
**Sprint:** Track 3, Week 15

##  Story Description
As an **International Sales Manager**, I want **region-specific business intelligence** so that I can **understand local market dynamics and competitive landscapes**.

##  Acceptance Criteria

### AC1: Regional Data Sources 
- Integrate regional business databases (Europe, Asia, LATAM)
- Local news sources per region
- Regional regulatory databases
- Local social media platforms

### AC2: Market-Specific Insights 
- Regional market size and growth rates
- Local competitor analysis
- Regulatory environment summary
- Economic indicators by region

### AC3: Currency & Timezone Handling 
- Multi-currency support (50+ currencies)
- Real-time exchange rates
- Timezone-aware data display
- Regional number formatting

### AC4: Regional Compliance 
- GDPR (Europe)
- CCPA (California)
- PIPEDA (Canada)
- Data residency requirements

**Owner:** James + International Team  
**Duration:** 2 weeks
