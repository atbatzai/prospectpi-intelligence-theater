#  Story 11.2: Predictive Sales Intelligence

**Epic:** 11 - Advanced Analytics & Reporting  
**Priority:** P1  
**Story Points:** 21  
**Sprint:** Track 4, Week 19

##  Story Description
As a **Sales Manager**, I want **AI-predicted deal outcomes** and **pipeline forecasting** so that I can **accurately forecast revenue and prioritize opportunities**.

##  Acceptance Criteria

### AC1: Deal Win Probability 
- ML model predicts deal close probability
- Factors: dossier usage, engagement, company signals
- Real-time probability updates
- 80%+ prediction accuracy

### AC2: Revenue Forecasting 
- 30/60/90 day revenue predictions
- Confidence intervals
- Historical accuracy tracking
- Scenario modeling

### AC3: Opportunity Prioritization 
- AI-ranked opportunity list
- Score based on: win probability, deal size, urgency
- Recommended next actions
- Integration with CRM opportunity stages

### AC4: Churn Prediction 
- Identify at-risk customers
- Churn probability score
- Early warning alerts
- Recommended retention actions
